The gap

You tab away. Claude finishes in 40 seconds. You find out twenty minutes later.

That's the gap NotifyMe closes. The moment a session goes from working to done, a native macOS notification tells you what Claude said — not just that it happened. One click puts you back in the exact tab. No polling a terminal that isn't ready. No forgotten agent blocked on your reply.

busy · Claude is working → ● done · you're notified → click · teleport to the tab
